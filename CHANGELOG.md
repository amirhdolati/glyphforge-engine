## 0.1.0

Initial standalone extraction from Glyph City: Afterlight.

- Colored variable-height grid ray caster with rectangular scene data.
- RendASCII-derived triangle backend with in-memory mesh API.
- ANSI full/delta rendering, generic input events and safe POSIX terminal ownership.
- HTML, SVG, ANSI and text export.
- Six runnable demos, actual frame previews and animated cube gallery.
- Installable source layout, tests, CI, provenance and third-party license notices.
